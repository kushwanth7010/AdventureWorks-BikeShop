# Interview Defense — Global Market Expansion & Profitability Strategy

## 60-second explanation
I used my AdventureWorks BI project as the analytical base and added a consulting layer. The business question was how to grow profitably across markets and products rather than simply reporting revenue. I structured the case around market attractiveness, portfolio economics, customer growth, pricing, and execution. The key insight was that the business is highly dependent on bikes for revenue, so I proposed protecting core markets while testing accessory cross-sell, market-specific pricing, and retention initiatives. I would measure incremental profit, attachment rate, repeat purchase, and return rate before scaling.

## Questions I should be ready to answer
### Why not recommend entering a new country immediately?
The current dataset is internal sales data. A genuine market-entry recommendation also needs market size, competitor intensity, regulation, distribution economics, and required investment.

### What is the main strategic risk?
Revenue concentration in one major category can expose the business to category-specific demand shocks and limits diversification.

### Why focus on incremental profit?
Revenue growth can destroy value if discounts, returns, or acquisition costs rise faster than gross profit.

### How would you test cross-sell?
Create a targeted customer cohort, offer relevant accessories after a bike purchase, compare attachment rate and contribution profit with a control cohort, and scale only if economics improve.

### What would you ask the client first?
Clarify the objective: growth, margin, market share, cash generation, or strategic presence. The answer changes the prioritization framework.
